Confirmed defect from source inspection and supplied live evidence
Location is intentionally omitted from the INSERT and bind_param in test-webapp.php. Previews show silay and Test for ids 2-3; the tester reports silay, bcd, and ambot for ids 4-6. The database stores NULL for all five, documenting five mismatches with preview evidence for the first two.

Title: Submitted Location is omitted from the database insertion.
Affected field: main_corrupt_politicians.Location
Evidence basis: Source inspection, user-supplied form/modal/database screenshots, and tester-reported expected Locations for ids 4-6. Five mismatches documented. Separate cancellation evidence confirms retained inputs and unchanged count of 6.
